Select a cell or a peak to hear its exact frequency. Use the inspector to play a nearby partial together with it.

In Pitch map, equal vertical distances represent equal musical intervals in cents, or equal frequency differences in Hz. Select a mark and use + or − to zoom around it. Closest pair frames the nearest partial from another source. Drag to pan, or pinch or Ctrl/⌘-scroll to zoom at a point.

Drag a column header to reorder it, or use the move controls in its settings. Group names can be edited there too.

In Tuning comparison, positive cents mean the selected partial is sharp of the reference. EDO steps count from A4. In Closest partials, positive cents mean the other source is higher. Click either comparison to hear both tones for six seconds.

Select an origin partial, then choose Link partial in the inspector. Its frequency sets the destination source’s fundamental. Red cords join linked partials. Unlink keeps the destination at its current frequency.

Each source uses the ideal harmonic series, fₙ = n × f₁. Peak heights are illustrative. All playback uses sine tones at sounding pitch.
